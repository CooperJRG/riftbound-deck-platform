The Best Decks out of Bologna

RQ Bologna saw all 28 Legends battle to supremacy, here’s the Best-Of decks and the Top 8!
Organized Play2026-02-25T17:00:00.000Z

With over 1,466 competitors in the main event, Riftbound saw all 28 Champions represented in Bologna, the first European RQ for the game! Just a week after the launch of Spiritforged, players had some idea of what to expect in the meta, but it’s fair to say that the results of the event were surprising.

Dominating the meta going into the event were the expected: Draven, Kai’Sa, and Irelia all had triple digit competitors playing with them. That said, Draven’s dominance was probably hampered by being public enemy #1 coming into the weekend, as well as the draw for Best-Of prizes.

However, when it came to Day 2 we began to see some significant shifts in the playing field.

Twenty-five of twenty-eight Champions made it to Day 2, a notable increase from RQ Houston where only ten of the 16 legal Champions made it to Day 2. Draven was the top performer in converting players to Day 2, likely due to the prevalence of off-Meta decks in the field. Miss Fortune Aurora decks remain quite strong and competitive in the meta, and it looks that Chaos rune decks are also doing well in the meta.

Speaking of Chaos Runes, Ezreal was the weekend’s breakout star with two copies making it into the Top 8 and, in the hands of Alanzq1, taking it all home in the finals.
