Overview

The Atlanta Regional Qualifier followed shortly after Lille as the second major Spiritforged event of the post-ban meta. It's also the final tournament before Set 3: Unleashed is released.

Over 1500 players competed over the weekend of April 24th 2026 to April 26th 2026.

If you're curious, check out our Tier List to see how the Atlanta meta compares to our rankings!
