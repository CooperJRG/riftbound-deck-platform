News

Overview

Bans are an unfortunate but inevitable part of pretty much every TCG. Oftentimes, they are a last resort effort to help fix an unbalanced format, or catch unwanted design mistakes.

This of course only applies to competitive formats. If you're sitting across the kitchen table, feel free to include whichever cards you like!

However, if you're looking to play at your local game store (or any higher level events), knowing which cards are banned is an absolute must. Don't worry, we've got you covered with our complete list.

New to the game and want to check out some cards? Have a look through our Card Gallery!
